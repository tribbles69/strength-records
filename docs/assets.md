# Site art log

All art was generated with Higgsfield on Aaron's credits, on 12 November 2026.

- **Cost:** 0.5 credits per image. **8 images, 4.0 credits in total.** Balance went from 53.75 to 49.75.
- **Rule followed:** site art only. Stylised linocut-style illustration of iron objects. No people, no lifts being performed, nothing that could be mistaken for a real lift or for record evidence.
- Eight further requests in the first batch were rejected by the service's rate limit before they started. They produced nothing and cost nothing.
