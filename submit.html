Submit a lift

No account needed. Read the rules and the standard for your lift first: a lift is judged only on its video.

Before you send it

  • One unedited take, with every plate shown on camera before the lift.
  • Your whole body and the bar in frame for the whole lift.
  • The video is public or unlisted, and will stay up.
  • It beats the standing record by at least 0.5 kg, or the table is empty.
The lift

The name shown on the record. An Instagram or Reddit handle, or any name you like.

Bar, plates and collars together. Pounds are converted and rounded down to the nearest 0.5 kg.

YouTube (public or unlisted) is preferred. Instagram is accepted.

Division

Every table is kept twice. Choose the one you are entering.

Show the scale at zero, then reading a marked plate, then step on.

One or two sentences, shown on the record. For example: "Left below-elbow amputee, lifting with a hook attachment." Say as much or as little as you want. This is information about your health or disability, and it is published only because you choose to give it.

Contact

Only used to tell you whether the lift was approved, or to ask a question. Leave it empty and you will not hear back, but an approved lift will still appear.
