    How it works

    1. Film one unedited take. Show every plate on the bar, then do the lift with your whole body and the bar in frame.
    2. Send the link. No account needed. A handle, the lift, the weight and your video link. Email is optional and never published.
    3. It is checked against the written standard. If it passes it goes on the board with your video. If not, you are told why.
    Read the rules Submit a lift

    Why this exists

    General record sites are too broad to moderate properly, and some sell categories. This registry does one thing: strength, on video, under rules you can read before you lift. Nobody pays to submit or to hold a record, and beaten records stay on the page as history.
