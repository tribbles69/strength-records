Privacy

This registry stores as little as it can. This page names all of it.

What is published

If your record is approved, the following is shown on this site and stored in the site's public code repository on GitHub:

  • your handle
  • the lift and the weight
  • your bodyweight, if you gave it, and how it was shown (weigh-in or self-reported)
  • your division, table and class
  • the date of the lift
  • your video link, and your weigh-in video link if you gave one
  • for adaptive entries: the category you chose and the description you wrote

Nothing else is published.

Adaptive entries

Your category and description say something about your health or disability. They are published only because you chose to submit them, in your own words. You can ask for them to be changed or removed at any time.

What is never published

Your email address. It is optional. If you give one it is used only to reply about your submission. It is never put on the site or in the repository, never shared, and never added to a mailing list.

Where your submission goes

The forms on this site are handled by Formspree, a company in the USA. Formspree receives what you typed, plus the technical details your browser sends with any web request, such as your IP address. It forwards your submission to the moderator by email and keeps a copy in the moderator's Formspree account.

Submissions are deleted from Formspree and from the moderator's inbox once they have been dealt with, and no later than 90 days after you sent them. Submissions that are declined are never published.

Your video

Your video stays on the service you uploaded it to, such as YouTube or Instagram, under your control. The registry links to it. It does not copy or store it. On a record page the video only loads when someone presses play, and playing it loads content from that service, which may set its own cookies.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as any web host does.

Changing or removing your record

Ask, and your record will be corrected or taken down. You do not need to give a reason. The site updates within minutes.

One thing to know: the site's repository is public and keeps a history of changes, so an earlier version of a removed record can remain in that history. Ask, and it will be removed from the history as well.
